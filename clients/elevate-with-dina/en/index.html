I don't have a big audience yet. Is this for me?

Yes. Everything starts with your value and your offer, not your follower count. A clear offer sells to a small audience too.

Do I need to have a program already?

No. If you have one, you'll learn to sell it better. If you don't, you'll shape your expertise into one.

Do I need to be good with technology or AI?

No. Everything is shown step by step, and the AI tools are there to save you time, not add work.
